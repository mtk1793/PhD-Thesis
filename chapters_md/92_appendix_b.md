# Appendix B: Test-Suite Summary

The Stage-1 rebuild is protected by a pytest regression suite that grew phase by phase with the code, reaching 52 passing tests at the completion of Phase 6, with no test skipped and no test marked as expected failure. The suite is the executable form of the verification discipline described in Chapter 10: every property that the results of Chapter 11 rely upon, from power-flow conservation to the semantics of the arbitration blend, is asserted by a named test that runs in seconds on a workstation. This appendix summarises the suite's structure, describes the categories of property it checks, and records how to reproduce the pass record.

The phase-by-phase structure of the suite is shown in Table B.1. Each phase's tests are cumulative, so the Phase 6 total of 52 comprises the data tests of Phase 1, the grid and device tests of Phase 2, the baseline and stability tests of Phase 3, the System 1 tests of Phase 4, the QIRL tests of Phase 5, and the arbiter tests of Phase 6. Phase 0 contributed no tests because it delivered the scaffold, the pinned dependencies, and the scripted data acquisition whose verification is procedural rather than assertive; its output is checked instead by the digest verification of Appendix A.

**Table B.1: Phase-by-phase composition of the pytest regression suite (52 tests passing).**

| Phase | Component | Tests (cumulative) | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 0 | Scaffold, dependency pinning, data download | 0 | environment setup and provenance, verified via digests |
| Phase 1 | OPSD loader and disaggregation | 8 | gap policy, split disjointness, penetration shares |
| Phase 2 | QSTS environment, FACTS models, EV fleet | 16 (8 data + 8 grid) | conservation checks, device effect isolation, SoC bounds |
| Phase 3 | Baseline controllers and stability | 22 | droop and PI semantics, loading margin, contingency response |
| Phase 4 | System 1 CNN-LSTM | 35 | encoder shape, action mapping, training determinism |
| Phase 5 | System 2 QIRL | 44 | amplitude normalisation, Born rule, tunneling rate |
| Phase 6 | Metacognitive Arbiter | 52 | blend weights, mode transitions, SAFE_MODE behaviour |

The categories of property checked by the suite deserve description, because they show that the tests assert physics and semantics rather than implementation trivia. The conservation checks verify that the power-flow solution balances active and reactive power at every bus to numerical precision, so any solver or model change that silently breaks physical consistency fails the suite before it can corrupt a result. The gap-policy tests pin the semantics of Chapter 2: gaps of two hours or less are linearly interpolated and flagged through the interpolation masks, longer gaps remain missing and excluded, and the post-policy completeness of the national load series is asserted exactly. The split-disjointness tests assert that the training, validation, and test periods share no timestamps, protecting every reported result from leakage.

The grid-side categories continue the same pattern. The convergence tests assert that every hourly power flow in a phase run converges, encoding the 100% convergence record of Chapter 11 as a pass or fail condition rather than an observation. The device-effect-isolation tests verify that each FACTS device changes the solution in exactly its intended way: the SVC and STATCOM through their shunt susceptance columns, the TCSC through the effective branch reactance under its compensation factor, and the UPFC through its shunt and series contributions, each asserted against a hand-computed reference case. The EV tests charge and discharge the fleet over horizons of at least twenty steps and assert that the SoC trajectories remain inside the [0.2, 0.9] bounds under the dispatch rule, which is the runtime guard for the invariance property proved in Chapter 3, and they check the asymmetric efficiency accounting for charging and discharging.

The controller-side categories protect the learning components. The System 1 tests fix the encoder's 160-feature output, the nine-action mapping and its bounds, and the determinism of behaviour-cloning training under the fixed seed, so a retrained model either matches or differs visibly. The QIRL tests assert that the amplitude vector stays unit-norm after every reinforcement update, that the Born-rule selection reproduces the expected distribution on controlled inputs, that the tunneling replacement rate matches its configured fraction, and that the candidate set respects the action box. The arbiter tests verify the blending equation at and around the threshold, including the smooth monotone behaviour of the weight as the stress metric crosses 0.03 p.u., the five controller modes, and the SAFE_MODE fallback. The contingency tests confirm the restoration property used in Section 11.3, namely that the post-event state returns to the pre-event state bit for bit, and the metrics tests assert that every run emits the complete metrics frame, so no controller can be benchmarked on a partial record.

Reproducing the pass record requires only the environment of Appendix A and the repository itself: from the repository root, the command pytest -q collects the six test modules and reports 52 passed. The suite completes in well under a minute because it operates on small synthetic cases and short profile windows rather than the full month runs. A contributor regenerating the suite after any change obtains either a green record, in which case the thesis's claims remain intact, or a named failure, in which case the offending property is identified before any result is produced.

The verification culture that this suite embodies is the final point worth recording. The suite was written alongside each phase rather than after it, it asserts properties that a reviewer can trace to a chapter, and it converts the thesis's most important invariance and feasibility claims into executable checks that will outlive the thesis itself. In a field where learned controllers are routinely published without executable evidence, the 52-test record, together with the digests and seeds of Appendix A and the campaign protocol of Chapter 10, constitutes the auditable backbone on which the results of Chapter 11 rest.
